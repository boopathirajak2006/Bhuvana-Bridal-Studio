Studio Terms

Terms & Cancellation Policy

Clear guidelines to protect your wedding date, provide a transparent experience, and ensure flawless bridal preparation and blouse tailoring.

Last Updated: October 2026 · Updated Version

1. Overview & Acceptance

These terms govern all services provided by Bhuvana's Bridal Studio (founder & artist: Bhuvaneshwari), based in Erumbalai, Steel Plant Road, Salem, Tamil Nadu, encompassing both divisions:

  • Bridal & Beauty Studio: Bridal makeup, HD makeup, ceremony makeup, hair styling, saree draping, pre-pleating, and mehndi artistry.
  • Designer Blouse Studio: Custom blouse designing, master tailoring, and handcrafted Aari needle embroidery.

By submitting an enquiry, confirming an order, or paying a booking advance, you agree to the policies outlined below.

2. Booking Confirmation & Date Reservation

  • Advance Deposit: Auspicious wedding dates, muhurtham hours, and tailoring workshop slots are reserved strictly upon receipt of an advance booking deposit. Enquiries via WhatsApp without a deposit do not guarantee date reservation.
  • Custom Quotation: All website amounts for makeup are confirmed promotional starting rates. Exact quotations depend on location, call time, headcount, and fabric/embroidery complexity for blouse orders.
  • Scope of Service: Agreed service details (looks, lash applications, saree drapes, blouse necklines, delivery date) are recorded in writing via WhatsApp consultation.

3. Bridal Makeup & Trials

  • Trials are scheduled by mutual appointment at our Salem studio to test shade matching and hairstyle compatibility.
  • Brides are requested to arrive with clean skin and dry hair. Please inform the artist of any known skin sensitivities or allergies prior to makeup application.
  • Call times for muhurthams are fixed to ensure full completion with ample buffer for morning ceremony rituals and photography.

4. Designer Blouse Orders & Fitting Policy

  • Measurements: Blouses are tailored to measurements provided directly by the client or measured from a well-fitting sample blouse.
  • Aari Work Timelines: Bridal Aari embroidery orders should be placed at least 15 to 30 days in advance to allow for precision drafting, hand-needlework, and lining assembly.
  • Alterations: Minor fitting adjustments are provided complimentary to guarantee an ideal fit before your wedding event.

5. Travel & Outstation Guidelines

  • Local Salem: Travel within Salem city limits, Steel Plant, and Erumbalai is covered or quoted transparently at booking.
  • Outstation Venues: For weddings outside Salem across Tamil Nadu, travel conveyance and overnight accommodation (if early morning call time is required) must be arranged or reimbursed as agreed in the quotation.

6. Cancellation & Refund Policy

Deposit Policy: Once your date or tailoring slot is confirmed, we turn away other clients for that specific slot. Consequently, advance booking deposits are non-refundable.
  • Client Cancellation: If you cancel your event or order, the advance deposit is forfeited to offset reserved studio time and blocked dates.
  • Rescheduling: If your event date changes, we will accommodate the new date subject to calendar and workshop availability.
  • Artist Emergency: In the rare event the artist is incapacitated due to unavoidable medical emergency, we will arrange a verified bridal replacement of equal caliber or refund 100% of monies paid.

7. Payment Modes

Payments are accepted via UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), Net Banking / IMPS, or Cash. Remaining balances are settled upon completion of services or at blouse trial delivery.
