Studio Terms

Terms & Cancellation Policy

Clear guidelines to protect your wedding date, provide a stress-free experience, and ensure flawless bridal preparation on your auspicious day.

Last Updated: October 2026

1. Overview & Acceptance

These terms govern all bridal makeup, hair styling, saree draping, bridal trials, and event makeup services provided by Bhuvana's Bridal Studio (artist: Bhuvaneshwari), based in Erumbalai, Steel Plant Road, Salem, Tamil Nadu.

By submitting a booking enquiry, paying a booking deposit, or confirming a session with us, you agree to the terms and policies set out below.

2. Booking Confirmation & Date Reservation

  • Deposit Requirement: Wedding dates and auspicious muhurtham hours are held strictly upon receipt of the agreed booking deposit. Verbal or WhatsApp inquiries without a deposit do not guarantee date exclusivity.
  • Written Quotation: All rates listed on our website are starting rates. A personalized written quotation will be provided upon review of your exact venue location, call time, and number of people getting ready.
  • Service Inclusions: The exact scope of service (makeup finish, skin prep, lash application, saree draping, hair styling, hair accessories) is mutually agreed and recorded in your booking quotation.

3. Bridal Trials

A bridal trial is designed to test skin compatibility, perfect your preferred look, and refine hairstyling options well in advance of the ceremony:

  • Trials are scheduled by mutual appointment at the studio or agreed location.
  • Feedback and look adjustments are noted carefully for replication on the wedding day.
  • Trial appointments must be booked in advance and are subject to artist studio availability.

4. Venue Conditions & Client Preparation

Getting-Ready Venue Requirements: Flawless bridal application requires a clean, well-lit workspace with good ventilation / air-conditioning, a mirror, accessible power outlets for styling tools, and a comfortable seating arrangement.
  • Skin & Hair Prep: Brides and family members should arrive with cleansed, moisturized skin (free of heavy oils) and clean, dry hair to allow on-time styling.
  • Allergies & Sensitivities: The client must notify the artist of any known skin allergies, sensitivities, or medical skin treatments prior to application. While we use high-grade professional cosmetics, we cannot be held liable for undisclosed allergic reactions.

5. Timelines & Punctuality

  • Muhurtham Timing: Auspicious ceremony hours are sacred and non-negotiable. The agreed call time ensures all styling is completed with time for photography before the muhurtham starts.
  • Client Delays: If the client or family members arrive late to the designated call time, the artist will make every professional effort to finish, but cannot compromise completion standards for the primary bride.

6. Travel & Outstation Policy

  • Local Salem Venues: Travel within Salem city limits and Erumbalai / Steel Plant is covered or quoted transparently at booking.
  • Outstation Venues: For weddings outside Salem or destination ceremonies across Tamil Nadu, travel conveyance (cab, fuel, or flight/train) and safe overnight accommodation (if early morning call time is required) must be arranged or reimbursed as agreed in the quotation.

7. Cancellation, Rescheduling & Refunds

Why deposits are non-refundable: Once your date and time slot are reserved, we decline all subsequent bride inquiries for that specific date. Consequently, booking deposits are non-refundable.
  • Client Cancellation: If you cancel your booking for any reason, the advance deposit is forfeited to compensate for the blocked date and lost business.
  • Rescheduling: If your wedding date changes, we will make every effort to accommodate the new date subject to artist availability. If the new date is unavailable, standard cancellation terms apply.
  • Artist Emergency: In the extremely rare and unforeseen event that the artist is incapacitated due to medical emergency, we will either provide an experienced, pre-approved replacement bridal artist of equivalent skill or immediately refund 100% of all monies received.

8. Payment Terms

  • Deposit: Due upon booking confirmation to secure the date.
  • Balance Payment: The remaining balance is payable prior to or on the wedding day before completion of styling services.
  • Accepted Modes: UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), Net Banking / IMPS, or Cash.
