The Studio Process

How Your Designer Blouse is Crafted

From initial saree fabric review to the final trial fitting.

1. Design & Fabric Consultation

Share your saree photos, border details, and preferred necklines. We suggest complementary color schemes, neck shapes, and sleeve lengths suited to your posture and style.

2. Aari Embroidery Stenciling

Your chosen motifs (temple, peacock, floral, or zardozi) are hand-drafted onto trace sheets and transferred onto the fabric for precision needlework by master artisans.

3. Precision Tailoring & Finish

After embroidery completion, the blouse is cut and stitched with premium soft lining, padded cups (if selected), reinforced neck piping, and handcrafted latkan tassels.

Common Questions

Blouse Studio FAQ

How much advance time is needed for bridal Aari work?

We recommend placing bridal Aari embroidery orders at least 15 to 30 days before your wedding or muhurtham date. This ensures sufficient time for intricate hand-needlework, stitching, and trial fittings.

Can I provide my own sample blouse for measurements?

Yes! You may provide a well-fitting sample blouse, or visit our Salem studio for personalized body measurements and posture assessment.

Can you match the Aari work with my saree border colors?

Absolutely. We closely match the zari color (antique gold, bright gold, copper, or silver) and thread shades directly to your saree’s pallu and border.

Do you offer urgent or express stitching?

Express stitching is available subject to workshop capacity and current order volumes. Please enquire via WhatsApp with your required date to verify availability.
