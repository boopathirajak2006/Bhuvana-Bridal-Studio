Privacy Policy

At Bhuvana's Bridal Studio, we treat every enquiry, phone call, and bridal schedule with the utmost discretion, care, and professional respect.

Effective Date: October 2026

1. Who We Are

This Privacy Policy outlines how Bhuvana's Bridal Studio (“we”, “us”, or “our”), founded and managed by bridal makeup artist Bhuvaneshwari, handles personal information collected through our website, phone consultations, and WhatsApp communications.

Our studio services Salem, Erumbalai, Steel Plant, and nearby areas, with travel available for wedding venues across Tamil Nadu and outstation destinations by arrangement.

2. Information We Collect

When you reach out for booking inquiries or schedule consultations, we collect only the necessary details required to deliver high-quality bridal services:

  • Client Contact Details: Your full name, mobile / WhatsApp phone number, and optional email address.
  • Event Specifics: Event date, makeup call time, getting-ready venue address / location in Salem or destination venue, and the total number of individuals requiring makeup (e.g., bride, bridesmaids, mother, family).
  • Styling Preferences: Selected services (Bridal HD/Airbrush, Trial, Engagement, Reception, Hairstyling, Saree Draping) and makeup finish preferences (e.g., matte, dewy, soft glam, traditional).
  • Direct Communications: Notes from WhatsApp chats, phone conversations, skin sensitivity considerations, and timeline discussions.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information exclusively for legitimate bridal service and customer support purposes:

  • To verify artist availability for your requested wedding or event date.
  • To prepare customized service quotes based on your exact location, guest count, and call time.
  • To communicate essential morning timelines, skin prep guidelines, and muhurtham coordination.
  • To conduct bridal trials and maintain personal notes on shade matches, skin sensitivities, and preferred hairstyling looks.
  • To maintain studio booking records, billing receipts, and deposit documentation.

4. Third-Party Services & Communication Platforms

Our enquiry flow uses WhatsApp Click-to-Chat (Meta Platforms, Inc.) to make communication quick and convenient. When you tap “Bridal Booking” or click our WhatsApp link, your pre-filled inquiry text is transferred to the WhatsApp application on your device. Your interactions on WhatsApp are subject to WhatsApp's own privacy policy and terms of service.

We do not sell, rent, lease, or monetize your personal information to any third-party brokers, advertisers, or marketing agencies.

5. Photographs, Social Media & Portfolio Usage

Our Client Discretion Promise: Bridal and trial photographs or behind-the-scenes videos are only shared on our professional Instagram (@__itz_berry_) or studio portfolio with your prior verbal or written consent. If you prefer that your bridal pictures remain strictly private, we will completely honor your wish.

6. Data Security & Retention

We implement appropriate physical, technical, and operational measures to protect your contact records from unauthorized access. Your booking information is retained only as long as necessary to complete your bridal event services, fulfill accounting obligations, and address any post-event inquiries.

7. Your Rights

Under applicable Indian legal standards and digital best practices, you have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal contact details we hold regarding your booking.
  • Request correction or updating of your contact details or wedding venue information.
  • Request the removal of any published portfolio photographs featuring you from our social channels.

8. Policy Updates & Contact Information

We may periodically update this policy to reflect operational improvements or regulatory requirements. Any updates will be posted directly to this page with an updated effective date.
