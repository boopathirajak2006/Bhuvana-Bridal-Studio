Privacy Policy

At Bhuvana's Bridal Studio, we treat every customer enquiry, consultation, and booking record with the utmost discretion, care, and professional respect.

Effective Date: October 2026 · Updated Version

1. Who We Are

This Privacy Policy outlines how Bhuvana's Bridal Studio (“we”, “us”, or “our”), founded and managed by Bhuvaneshwari, handles personal information collected through our website, phone consultations, and WhatsApp communications across both our service divisions: Bridal & Beauty Studio and Designer Blouse Studio.

Our studio is based in Salem, Tamil Nadu (serving Erumbalai, Steel Plant, and nearby areas), with on-location travel available for wedding venues across Salem and surrounding districts by arrangement.

2. Information We Collect

When you submit an enquiry form or message us for quotes, we collect only the necessary details required to deliver customized bridal makeup and designer blouse services:

  • Client Contact Details: Your full name, mobile / WhatsApp phone number, and optional email address.
  • Bridal & Event Specifics: Event date, makeup call time, getting-ready venue address / location in Salem, and the total count of individuals requiring makeup.
  • Designer Blouse Specifications: Required delivery date, saree/fabric type, selected blouse services (designing, stitching, Aari work), and measurement or neckline preferences.
  • Styling Preferences: Selected looks (HD, Airbrush, Traditional, Soft Glam) and henna/styling choices.
  • Direct Communications: Notes shared via WhatsApp, reference photographs of sarees or hairstyles, and timeline details.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information exclusively for legitimate studio service operations:

  • To verify artist and tailoring schedule availability for your requested date.
  • To prepare accurate, transparent quotations based on your service choices and venue location.
  • To coordinate morning preparation schedules, saree pre-pleating, and muhurtham timings.
  • To conduct trial sessions and record custom shade matches, skin sensitivities, or fitting measurements.
  • To maintain order documentation, booking deposits, and receipt records.

4. WhatsApp Click-to-Chat Integration

Our website uses WhatsApp Click-to-Chat to make contacting the studio effortless. When you tap submit on any enquiry form, your message details are pre-formatted and transferred to WhatsApp on your device. Your interactions on WhatsApp are subject to WhatsApp's standard privacy policies and encryption.

We do not sell, rent, lease, or distribute your personal contact information to any third-party marketing companies.

5. Client Discretion & Social Media Usage

Our Privacy Commitment: Client photographs or makeover videos are only shared on our Instagram profile (@__itz_berry_) or studio portfolio with your prior verbal or written permission. If you prefer that your bridal pictures remain strictly private, we will honor your preference unconditionally.

6. Data Security & Retention

We take reasonable precautions to safeguard your contact numbers and event records. Information is retained only as long as necessary to complete your wedding services, fulfill accounting records, or assist with follow-up orders.
